Common Questions

How much space is needed for the dome?
The dome requires a clear floor area of at least 7m x 7m and a ceiling height of 4m. School halls or gyms are usually perfect.
Is the dome wheelchair accessible?
Yes! Our mobile planetarium is designed with a zip entrance that allows for easy wheelchair access.
What happens if it rains during a telescope night?
Safety first! If weather doesn't permit outdoor viewing, we switch to an indoor virtual night sky tour inside the dome at no extra cost.
