PLANNING YOUR VISIT

Dome Setup &
Logistical Requirements

To ensure a safe and spectacular experience, our mobile planetarium dome requires specific indoor spaces. Please review these physical guidelines before booking your school assembly or group outreach event.

View School Packages Check Availability

Floor Dimensions

Requires a flat, clean indoor space of at least 7m x 7m (24ft x 24ft). Gymnasiums, assemblies, or large common rooms are ideal.

Ceiling Clearance

A minimum vertical clearance of 4m (13ft) is mandatory. Please ensure no low-hanging lights or fans are in the zone.

Power Source

Needs 1 standard 110V/120V electrical outlet within 50 feet of the setup. We provide all extension cords and safety covers.

ADA & Access

Fully wheelchair accessible with flat-threshold zip entry. Requires step-free path (elevator or ground level) from unloading area.

Seating Capacity

Comfortably accommodates up to 35 students (K-12) or 25 adults per 30-45 minute presentation. Rapid entry/exit designs allow back-to-back classes for school-wide visits.
