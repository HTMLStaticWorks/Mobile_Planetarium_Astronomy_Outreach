Dr. Julian Cosmos

A Journey Started in the Stars

Stellar was born from a simple observation: not every child has the opportunity to visit a major city observatory. Our founder, Dr. Julian Cosmos, spent a decade in research before realizing his true calling was outreach.

"I wanted to bring the universe to the doorstep of every school. Science shouldn't be confined to textbooks—it should be felt, seen, and experienced in 360 degrees."
